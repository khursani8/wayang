# storytelling template

Two hosts act out a short narrative across three scenes. Emotions are
sprinkled through the script and mood cards carry the scene.

Honesty note: emotions change the drawn character art only. With
`use_images: false` (placeholder boxes) they are invisible. To see them,
add emotion PNGs (`<emotion>_open.png`, `<emotion>_close.png`) under
`assets/images/<character_id>/` and set `use_images: true`.
